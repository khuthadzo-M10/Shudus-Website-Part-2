ABOUT US

Reliable Plumbing & Construction Services

Shudus Plumbing and Construction (Pty) Ltd is a South African plumbing and construction company incorporated in 2017. The company was established as an emerging supplier of products and services, with the aim of providing clients with quality, efficient and economical solutions.

The company specialises primarily in plumbing and general building services and operates mainly in Gauteng, with the capacity and intention to provide services at clients' preferred locations across South Africa and neighbouring countries.

Shudus Plumbing and Construction serves property developers, private businesses, individuals, corporate organisations, municipalities and government departments.
