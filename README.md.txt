INDEX / HOME PAGE
--------------------
Purpose:
The Index page is the main landing page of the website. Its purpose is to
create a strong first impression, introduce the company and quickly guide
visitors to the most important parts of the website.

ABOUT US PAGE
----------------
Purpose:
The About Us page explains who Shudus Plumbing and Construction is and builds
trust by providing background information about the organisation.
What happens on this page:
- The company name is introduced:
  Shudus Plumbing and Construction (Pty) Ltd.
- The history of the organisation is explained.
- Visitors learn that the company was incorporated in 2017.
- The company is described as a South African plumbing and construction
  company providing quality, efficient and economical solutions.
- The main areas of specialisation are explained, including plumbing and
  general building services.
- The company's operating area and target markets are explained.
- Previous project experience can be presented, including documented work
  associated with Fleurhof, Jabulani RDP housing projects and other
  residential developments.
- The Mission Statement explains the company's commitment to reliable,
  affordable and high-quality plumbing and construction services.
- The Vision Statement explains the company's aim to become a trusted and
  recognised plumbing and construction company in South Africa.
- The target audience can also be introduced:
  * Property developers
  * Private businesses
  * Individuals/Homeowners
  * Corporate organisations
  * Municipalities and government departments


SERVICES PAGE
----------------
Purpose:
The Services page explains exactly what the company offers. Services should

- Plumbing Installation
- Plumbing Repairs

- Property Development Plumbing

CONTACTS / CONTACT US PAGE
-----------------------------
Purpose:
The Contact page makes it easy for customers to communicate with Shudus
Plumbing and Construction.

What happens on this page:
- A short introduction encourages customers to contact the company for
  plumbing and construction services.
- Telephone contact information is displayed clearly.
- Email contact information is displayed.
- The company's physical address/location is displayed.
- Business operating hours are shown.
- Emergency service information can be provided.
- A message/contact form allows visitors to send an enquiry or request
  a callback.
- A map/location section can show where the organisation is located.
- WhatsApp/contact buttons should be easily accessible, especially on
  mobile devices.
- The footer provides additional quick links, services, contacts,
  social media links and copyright information.

GET A QUOTE / REQUEST A QUOTE PAGE
-------------------------------------
Purpose:
The Get a Quote page is designed to generate qualified customer enquiries
and potential business opportunities.

What happens on this page:
The visitor completes a quotation request form.

The form should collect:
- Full Name
- Company Name
- Phone Number
- Email Address
- Location / Area
- Service Required
- Type of Property
- Preferred Date
- Description of Work
- Upload Photos / Documents, where applicable

The visitor then selects:
- Residential
- Commercial
- Development
- Other

The visitor submits the form using:
"SUBMIT QUOTE REQUEST"
